ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Turning Surplus Food Into Meaningful Impact

The Smart Food Waste Reduction and Surplus Food Management System is designed to reduce unnecessary food waste by connecting food providers with recipients.

Providers can record surplus food, recipients can discover available food, and the system can track donations and waste reduction.

  • ✓ Reduce avoidable food waste
  • ✓ Connect surplus food with recipients
  • ✓ Track food donation and waste
  • ✓ Provide data-driven insights
